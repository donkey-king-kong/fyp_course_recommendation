## Conclusion and stop point

- The full-catalog pair **does** establish that pass-1 assignment masks a plausible explicit-interest option in an AI/ML case. The Cloud pair is a counterexample to a simplistic "weak winner means no career fit" rule: `SC3030` is a direct candidate behind the higher-scored `SC3050`. Keep the owner's course-value decision, but inspect the eligible candidate frontier before declaring an open slot.
- A **genuine relevance-based open-slot result remains unobserved**: the unchanged policy still fills all these slots. AI/ML `SC3021` depth after completed AI/ML courses is uncertain, and the other low-score alternatives must be audited for retrieval, semantic tags and eligibility. Do not choose a numeric threshold to manufacture abstentions or alter evaluator/labels to claim success.
- Eligibility remains a separate guardrail. The recommendation request does not accept transcript-completed AU or validate standing, offering, cohort, co-requisites and capacity. The earlier `SC3060` no-completions fixture returning `ready` despite catalog Year 2 standing is still an application/fixture gap. `SC4001`'s neural-network tag being interpreted as computer networking and stale Cloud label paths are separate data/evidence findings. The new `SC3030` observation is a **ranking/policy mismatch**, not proof of a data defect.
- Stop here for review. Before a separate weak-fit implementation work unit, examine the candidate frontier for the AI/ML Y4-end case, resolve the eligibility coverage relevant to any interest pick, and define what the response/UI should say for genuinely open slots. Compare the later policy against all 24 frozen cases, 180 grid cells, positioned/counterfactual checks and every new open slot. No current metric or fill count was changed.
