## Proposed decision rule, not a numeric threshold

1. Apply the existing supported completion, duplicate, fixed-course, code-prefix, slot/programme and prerequisite-readiness filters before ranking either category. Treat unresolved offering/co-requisite/capacity checks as unknown, not as a reason to claim a course is irrelevant.
2. Require a **course-content-supported, role-specific learning contribution** to call a career match strong. Direct coverage of a key job skill or a well-evidenced supporting skill can qualify; an incidental keyword, an ambiguous tag, a weak indirect pathway or faculty/current/specialisation bonuses alone cannot. Check what is genuinely new after completed courses. Rank and allocate strong eligible career candidates first. This is a proposed evidence rule, not an instruction to require a fixed score of 5, 10, 15 or any other number.
3. For still-open slots, require an explicit student preference with a semantically matching course topic and useful new learning, then the **same** eligibility and duplicate checks. Label the result as an interest alternative, never as career evidence. An unrelated but strong career choice does not have to match the declared interest.
4. If neither category has an eligible, evidenced match, leave the slot open and explain the checked reason: no relevant candidate in the searched set, candidates excluded by a supported rule, or insufficient evidence requiring manual confirmation. Do not collapse unknown eligibility, retrieval failure and genuine weak fit into one "no match" message. Never use score or fill rate alone to force a candidate.

This rule resolves the priority and **qualitative** minimum fit. It does not calibrate a universal numeric threshold: role mappings and keyword/tag quality differ, and `SC4001`'s false networking path would contaminate threshold experiments. The existing `CAREER_FIT_FULL_PREFERENCE_SCORE=15` scales preference boosts; `MIN_ASSIGNED_RECOMMENDATION_SCORE=10` (BDE 5) is an assignment floor, **not** an approved strong-career boundary. Keep both unchanged in this review. For a later implementation proposal, compare all 24 frozen cases, the seven-case packet, full six-career grid, positioned and counterfactual checks, and every newly open slot.
